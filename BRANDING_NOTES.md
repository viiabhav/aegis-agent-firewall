# AEGIS branding notes

AEGIS uses its own product name, interface copy, icon treatment and visual system.

For the hackathon submission, the application uses only factual text attribution:

- Built by Vaibhav Patil
- Team Beyond Tokens
- ET AI Hackathon 2026 — Agentic Edition
- Presented by Accenture

Third-party event/organizer logos, promotional artwork and campaign slogans are intentionally not embedded in the AEGIS product UI. Keep the official hackathon artwork in submission materials only where the organizer explicitly permits or provides it for participant use.

This note documents the project's branding approach; it is not legal advice.
